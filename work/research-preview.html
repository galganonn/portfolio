What We Did

Day Mapping

In each of the 18 interviews we asked reps to reconstruct their typical workday in 30–60 minute blocks — first hour, between meetings, end of day. The pattern repeated across almost every session.

Salesforce Activity Report

We pulled a standard report from Salesforce showing the timestamp of every call vs. the next CRM update. The data was already there — no one had looked at it this way before.
